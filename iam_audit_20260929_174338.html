## Issue 2: AdministratorAccess Policy
**Risk:** Excessive privilege; violates principle of least privilege

**Remediation:**
1. **Determine actual job function** - What does this user actually need to do?
2. **Remove AdministratorAccess** - Detach policy immediately
3. **Create/assign role-based policy** with minimum required permissions (e.g., `PowerUserAccess` + specific service permissions)
4. **Use permission boundaries** - Apply a boundary limiting maximum permissions
5. **Document justification** - If full admin access is genuinely required, document approval and implement time-based reviews (quarterly)

**Quick win:** If this is a human user, replace with `PowerUserAccess` + specific service restrictions rather than full admin.
